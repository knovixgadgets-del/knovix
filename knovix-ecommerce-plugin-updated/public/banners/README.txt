HOME BANNER PICTURES
--------------------
Put your real banner pictures in this folder (wide landscape, about 1600 x 700, JPG/PNG/WEBP).
Then open src/data/homeBanners.js and change  image: '/banners/dummy-banner-1.svg'
to        image: '/banners/your-file.jpg'

The three dummy-banner-*.svg files are placeholders - delete them once you add your own.
